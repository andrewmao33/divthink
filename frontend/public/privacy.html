Privacy Policy

divthink · last updated 16 September 2026

divthink is a canvas for branching conversations with AI models. This page explains what the service stores, who it shares data with, and how to have it deleted.

What is stored

  • Your account: the email address and account identifier provided by Google when you sign in.
  • Your canvases: the prompts you write, the replies the AI models return, the connections between them, and their positions.
  • Your API keys: encrypted before being stored. They are decrypted only to generate your replies, are never displayed again, and are never sent to your browser.
  • Timestamps for the items above.

What is not stored

  • No passwords: sign-in is handled by Google.
  • No payment details: you use your own AI provider account.
  • No advertising or analytics trackers.

Who your data is shared with

  • Anthropic or Google, depending on the model you choose: your prompts and the earlier messages of that conversation are sent to generate a reply, using your own API key. Their terms and privacy policies then apply to that request.
  • Service providers that run divthink: Vercel (website), Railway (server), and Supabase (database and sign-in).
  • Nobody else. Your data is not sold or used for advertising, and other signed-in users cannot see your canvases.

Your browser

Your sign-in session and the model you last picked are kept in your browser's local storage. No advertising or tracking cookies are used.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Your canvases stay until you delete them in the app.
  • You can remove a saved API key at any time from the menu under “API keys”.
  • To delete your account and everything in it, contact us (below) from the email address you signed in with.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. API keys are encrypted at rest with a key held separately from the database. Database access is restricted so that only the divthink server can read your data. No service can promise perfect security, so please use a provider API key with spending limits.

Children

divthink is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it. Significant changes will be noted on this page.
